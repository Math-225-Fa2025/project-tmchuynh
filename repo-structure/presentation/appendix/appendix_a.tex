\subsubsection{A.5 Analytical
Commentary}\label{a.5-analytical-commentary}

\textbf{Summary of Findings:}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  There is a measurable \textbf{rating offset} between online (Lichess)
  and official (FIDE) distributions, reflecting the structural
  differences in rating systems.
\item
  \textbf{Win rates} are relatively stable across rating groups, with a
  mild upward trend among higher-rated users, consistent with greater
  consistency and lower variance.
\item
  The \textbf{number of games played} increases modestly with rating,
  implying experience and activity correlate with performance
  sustainability.
\item
  The \textbf{performance gap} metric provides a proxy for inflation or
  competitive bias --- a useful baseline for future normalization across
  rating systems.
\end{enumerate}
